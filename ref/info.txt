Pocket Scout FC is a casual idle football team simulation game where every great club begins with a single scout.
Discover new players, build your squad, improve your team power, and watch your club battle through exciting football matches. Scout fresh talent, upgrade your lineup, strengthen your tactics, and push your team toward bigger wins.
Every match is quick, readable, and full of football action. Your squad keeps growing as you unlock stronger players, improve positions, and create the best team possible.

Can you turn a small football club into a legendary squad?

Features
Scout and collect football players
Build and upgrade your dream squad
Watch fast idle football matches
Improve your team power and tactics
Unlock stronger players as you progress
Lead your club through match after match
Simple, casual, and satisfying football gameplay

Scout smart. Build strong. Win big.
